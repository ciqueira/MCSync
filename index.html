Google Drive data

Your files move directly between Google Drive and your Mac.

MCSync requests either read-only or read-and-write Google Drive access, according to the option you select. OAuth tokens are stored locally in an encrypted rclone configuration. MCSync does not upload files, previews, metadata, or credentials to MC or Nexus servers.
